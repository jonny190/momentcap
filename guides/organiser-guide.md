# Organiser guide

This guide covers everything you need to run MomentCap for your event.

## Signing in

Go to `/admin` on your MomentCap address and enter the admin password you were given.

## Setting up the event

On the **Overview** page:

1. Type the event name. This is what guests see in big letters at the top of the camera screen.
2. Write a short message for guests if you like, for example "Snap away, we'd love to see the day through your eyes".
3. Leave both switches on:
   - **Accept new photos and videos** lets guests upload.
   - **Let guests see the album** lets guests browse everything that has been shared.
4. Click **Save changes**.

## Getting the QR code out

The Overview page shows your guest QR code and link. You can:

- **Print a sign.** This makes an A5 table card with the event name and the QR code. Print it straight from your browser, or save it as a PDF and send it to a printer.
- **Download PNG or SVG** to put the code on your own designs, such as invitations, menus or place cards. SVG stays sharp at any size.
- **Copy link** to share it in a group chat or by email.

Put a few signs around the venue: at the entrance, on each table and by the bar.

## During the event

Photos and videos show up in the **Album** within a few seconds. Videos take a little longer while the preview is prepared.

If something turns up that you'd rather guests didn't see, select it and click **Hide from guests**. Hidden items stay in your album and still come with the download, they just disappear from the guest view. **Delete** removes something for good.

## After the event

1. Turn off **Accept new photos and videos**. The QR code keeps working, but guests will see that the album has stopped taking new photos.
2. Use **Download everything (ZIP)** to save every original, exactly as it came off each guest's phone. Inside the ZIP, photos and videos are in separate folders, and each file is named with the time it was taken and the guest's name.
3. Leave **Let guests see the album** on for as long as you want people to be able to look back and download their favourites.

## If the link gets out

If the guest link ends up somewhere it shouldn't, click **Make a new link** on the Overview page. The old link and any printed QR codes stop working straight away, so you'll need to print new signs.
