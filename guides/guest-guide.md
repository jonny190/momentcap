# Guest guide

A short explainer you can share with guests, or adapt for a sign or invitation.

## How to share your photos

1. Point your phone camera at the QR code and tap the link that appears.
2. Tap the big gold button to take a photo, or **Video** to record a clip.
3. That's it. Your shot is sent while you carry on enjoying the event.

You don't need an app or an account.

## Good to know

- **Photos you've already taken** can be added too. Tap **Add photos you already took** and pick as many as you like.
- **Your name** is optional. Tap **Add your name to your shots** if you'd like the couple or hosts to know who took what. Your phone remembers it next time.
- **Bad signal?** Keep shooting. Everything waits on your phone and sends as soon as it can. If you close the page before it's finished, just open the link again later and it'll carry on.
- **The album** button shows everything everyone has shared. Tap a photo to see it full size or download it.
- **Changed your mind?** Open one of your own shots in the album and tap **Remove**.
